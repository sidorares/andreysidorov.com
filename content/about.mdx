I’ve been writing software since 1997, and my career path has taken some spectacularly strange turns.
My code has lived in avionics simulators, CAD systems, and on the International Space Station via a NASA and ESA contractor.
I’ve built systems for Yandex, navigated the chaos of Australian startups, and scaled enterprise platforms.

I have a habit of needing to understand things at the lowest possible level.
If I'm using a protocol, I usually end up reading the entire technical
specification. That specific brand of curiosity has led to
[77 open-source packages](https://www.npmjs.com/~sidorares) over the years.
Some of them are heavily relied upon by the industry, like
[json-bigint](https://github.com/sidorares/json-bigint) (25 million weekly
downloads) and [node-mysql2](https://github.com/sidorares/node-mysql2)
(11 million weekly downloads). Others are pure mad science, such as a
[full X11 window system written entirely in JavaScript](https://github.com/sidorares/node-x11),
and a [React renderer built specifically for X Window](https://github.com/sidorares/react-x11)
just to see if it could be done. For a few years, I also ran the
[Melbourne Node.js Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/NodeMelbourne/). We held
43 events for a community of 2,300 members, where I regularly gave talks on
React internals, the Node event loop, and machine learning.
