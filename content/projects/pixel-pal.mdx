Pixel Pal started as a way to sketch sprites without opening Aseprite. It's
~6kb of TypeScript over a `<canvas>`, with keyboard shortcuts borrowed from
the tools I missed.

## Why

Sometimes you just want to draw a 16×16 mushroom on the bus.

## Notable bits

- Palette presets stored in `localStorage`
- PNG export at any integer scale
- Undo stack with constant-memory snapshots
